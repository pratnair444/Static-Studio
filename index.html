// 01 — The Position

WHY I
STARTED THIS

What I Do

Plainly stated

I look at brands the way most people look at things they can't stop thinking about. What's actually working. What's quietly bleeding. What nobody's saying out loud yet.

Then I try to name those things clearly — even when it's not what someone was expecting to hear.

  • Identify what your brand actually communicates vs. what you think it does
  • Find the gap between the audience you have and the audience you deserve
  • Build marketing that leaves a mark, not just an impression
  • Say the thing that's true, even if it's inconvenient

What I Actually Believe

The honest version

Your brand narrative doesn't wait for you. The market writes it the moment you stop. And the version they write is rarely the one you wanted.

The brands that last aren't just reacting to culture — they're moving before it does. That takes a genuine point of view, not just a content calendar.

  • Reach is rented. Recall is owned. Optimise accordingly
  • Own a feeling, not just a category
  • Audiences can smell the absence of a real point of view
  • Every touchpoint is a vote for what your brand actually stands for
